HOW TO PLAY

Learn in short, focused rounds

  1. Choose a mode. Play a ten-question sprint, complete the full assessment in five blocks, or practise one learning area.
  2. Answer within 20 seconds. Use the buttons or press keys 1–4. A faster correct answer earns a small bonus.
  3. Read the explanation. Every answer includes the correct option, the reasoning and an applied takeaway.
  4. Use checkpoints. The full assessment pauses every ten questions so you can review progress or continue later.
  5. Review your evidence. Final results show accuracy by learning area, not only a total score.

Your active session is stored only in this browser. It is not sent anywhere.
